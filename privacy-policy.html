Privacy Policy - Auto Flow Veo - Batch Image/Video Creator

Last Updated: November 18, 2025

Introduction

This privacy policy describes how the "Auto Flow Veo - Batch Image/Video Creator" Chrome extension ("we", "our", or "the extension") handles user data. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about our data practices.

Data Collection

We do NOT collect:

  • Personal information
  • Browsing history (except for Google Flow/Veo3 pages)
  • Passwords or authentication credentials
  • Credit card or payment information
  • Location data
  • Data from other websites

Data stored locally on your device:

  • Extension settings (iteration count, delays, start index)
  • Prompts list
  • Workflow state
  • Language preferences

All data is stored locally in your browser's storage and is never transmitted to any external server.

Permissions Usage

Storage Permission

Purpose: To save your extension settings and prompts locally on your device.

Data stored: All data is stored locally in your browser's storage. No data is sent to any external server.

Security: All data remains on your local device and is not shared with any third parties.

Tabs Permission

Purpose: To automatically find and interact with Google Flow/Veo3 tabs for automation purposes.

Scope: The extension only accesses tabs with URLs containing "flow.google.com" or "labs.google.com". It does not read content from other tabs or access any unrelated websites.

Security: The extension does not access or read content from tabs outside of Google Flow/Veo3 domains.

Scripting Permission

Purpose: To inject automation scripts into Google Flow/Veo3 pages to enable batch creation functionality.

Scope: Scripts are only injected into flow.google.com and labs.google.com domains. No scripts are injected into other websites.

Security: The extension only operates on the specified Google Flow/Veo3 domains and does not modify or interact with any other websites.

Host Permissions

Purpose: To enable the extension to work on Google Flow/Veo3 platforms.

Domains accessed: *.flow.google.com and labs.google.com only.

Security: The extension does not access or collect data from any other websites or domains.

Downloads Permission

Purpose: To automatically download generated images and videos from Google Flow/Veo3.

Scope: The extension only downloads files that you explicitly generate through the extension's automation features.

Notifications Permission

Purpose: To notify you when batch operations are complete or if errors occur.

Scope: Notifications are only shown for extension-related events and do not contain any personal information.

Data Sharing

We do NOT share your data with:

  • Third-party services
  • Analytics services
  • Advertising networks
  • Any external servers
  • Any other parties

All data remains on your local device and is never transmitted externally.

Data Security

  • The extension only operates on Google Flow/Veo3 domains
  • No personal information is collected
  • No data is transmitted to external servers
  • All data is stored locally on your device
  • No data is shared with third parties

Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over your data. You can:

  • Clear extension data at any time through Chrome's extension settings
  • Uninstall the extension, which will remove all locally stored data
  • Review and modify your settings within the extension

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy. You are advised to review this privacy policy periodically for any changes.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or our data practices, please contact us at: support@annecao.ca
