Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 19, 2025

TL;DR (Too Long; Didn't Read):

Auto Flow Veo Extension does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All your settings and prompts are stored locally on your device. We respect your privacy.

1. Introduction

Auto Flow Veo Extension ("we", "our", or "the Extension") is a Chrome browser extension designed to automate batch image and video creation on Google Flow/Veo3. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.

2. Information We Collect

None. We do not collect any personal information, usage data, or analytics.

What is stored locally:

  • Your extension settings (repeat count, delays, start index)
  • Your prompt lists
  • Workflow state (for pause/resume functionality)
  • Language preference (Vietnamese/English)

✓ All data is stored locally using Chrome's storage API

✓ No data is sent to external servers

✓ No data is shared with third parties

3. Permissions Explained

The Extension requires certain Chrome permissions to function. Here's why:

activeTab

Purpose: Interact with the currently active Google Flow/Veo3 tab

Usage: Send automation commands to the active tab

Data Access: None - only interacts with flow.google.com pages

storage

Purpose: Save your settings and prompt lists

Usage: Store preferences locally on your device

Data Access: Local only - never transmitted

downloads

Purpose: Automatically download generated media

Usage: Save images/videos to your Downloads folder

Data Access: Media files from Flow/Veo3 only

tabs

Purpose: Find Google Flow/Veo3 tabs

Usage: Locate tabs with flow.google.com or labs.google URLs

Data Access: Only tab URLs, no content

scripting

Purpose: Inject automation scripts

Usage: Automate prompt filling and button clicking on Flow/Veo3

Data Access: Only on flow.google.com and labs.google domains

notifications

Purpose: Show completion notifications

Usage: Alert you when batch processing is complete

Data Access: None

4. Data Usage

The Extension uses your data solely for its intended functionality:

  • Storing settings for your convenience
  • Automating prompt submission to Google Flow/Veo3
  • Downloading generated media to your device
  • Tracking batch processing progress

We do NOT:

  • ❌ Send your prompts or settings to any server
  • ❌ Track your usage or behavior
  • ❌ Collect analytics or telemetry
  • ❌ Share data with third parties
  • ❌ Use cookies or tracking technologies
  • ❌ Access your personal information

5. Third-Party Services

The Extension interacts with Google Flow/Veo3 (flow.google.com and labs.google) to automate media generation. Your use of Google Flow/Veo3 is subject to Google's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

We are not affiliated with Google. This is an independent automation tool.

6. Data Security

All data is stored securely using Chrome's built-in storage API:

  • Data is encrypted by Chrome
  • Data is sandboxed per extension
  • Data is only accessible by the Extension
  • Content Security Policy (CSP) is enforced

7. Data Retention

Your data is retained until:

  • You manually clear it in the Extension
  • You uninstall the Extension
  • You clear Chrome's extension data

No data persists after uninstallation.

8. Children's Privacy

The Extension is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

Continued use of the Extension after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Your Rights

Since we don't collect any data, there is no data to access, correct, or delete. You can:

  • Clear Extension storage via Chrome settings
  • Uninstall the Extension at any time
  • Review the source code (open source)
